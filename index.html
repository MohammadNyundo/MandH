Our Story

M&H Boutique Hotel invites you into a world of understated luxury and authentic Kenyan hospitality, where each visit becomes a treasured memory and every guest becomes part of our story.

At the helm is Margret Mweya, founder and Managing Director, whose career in hospitality and hotel bookings spans decades. Her understanding of what guests need, and her eye for detail, shape every stay.

For Margret, M&H is the fulfilment of a cherished dream and a legacy for her children, grandchildren and the generations to come. She has poured her heart into it.
