Just outside the lively tourist center of Diani Beach, you’ll find an oasis of peace and relaxation.

With an oversized swimming pool, a cozy bar, and a restaurant with a large terrace, you’ll have everything you need for a restful and unforgettable holiday far from the rush of everyday life.
