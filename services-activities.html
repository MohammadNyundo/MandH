Services & Activities

Crafting unforgettable stays, one guest at a time.

Coastal experence & advanture

At M&H Boutique Hotel, the art of relaxation begins the moment you arrive. Nestled in a serene, oasis-like
compound, our property invites you to embrace the simple luxury of doing nothing — just unwinding by the pool,
letting the sea breeze drift through the palms, and soaking in the tranquility that surrounds you.

For those seeking adventure beyond our gates, Diani Beach offers a vibrant menu of activities that we can
arrange directly from our reception. Glide across turquoise waters with kite surfing or sailing, discover colourful
marine life while snorkelling, or cast a line on a deep-sea fishing trip. For golfers, an exceptional course is just 15
minutes away, while culture and nature lovers can embark on safaris and explore the wildlife in bora bora, also
explore the enchanting waters of Wasini Island, or wander through the historic streets of Mombasa’s Old Town.
Every experience is curated to ensure your time here is nothing short of unforgettable.

Impeccable services & amenities

Our philosophy at M&H Boutique Hotel is simple: every detail matters. That’s why our services are designed to
ensure comfort, convenience, and indulgence throughout your stay. Enjoy breakfast, lunch, and dinner whenever
you desire — our kitchen is open all day, ready to delight you with freshly prepared dishes.

Our reception team is at your service for bookings, recommendations, and personal assistance, while poolside
beds and plush towels await you after a refreshing swim. Sip a cocktail at our inviting bar, unwind on the
spacious terrace, and stay connected with complimentary Wi-Fi across the property.

Choose from elegantly appointed hotel rooms or our exclusive one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments, each
designed for a luxurious coastal living experience. For business gatherings, our modern conference room
provides a professional yet inspiring setting.
