Our story

M&H Boutique Hotel

Opened in 2024, this exclusive retreat was conceived as a sanctuary for the discerning traveller: a place where the vibrant spirit of Diani Beach meets the tranquility of a private, world-class escape.

Every element, from the refined architecture to the thoughtful amenities, has been designed to inspire relaxation, connection and unforgettable moments.

The founder

At the helm is Margret Mweya, founder and Managing Director, whose career in hospitality and hotel bookings spans decades. Margret’s deep understanding of guest expectations, coupled with her unwavering attention to detail, has shaped M&H into a destination where service is as warm as the coastal sun and every experience is elevated with grace.

For Margret, this hotel is more than an enterprise. It is the fulfilment of a cherished dream and the foundation of a lasting legacy for her children, grandchildren and generations to come.

She has poured her heart into creating an environment where every guest feels not only welcomed, but truly valued.

Your stay

Here, your mornings might begin with a leisurely breakfast prepared with the freshest local ingredients. Days unfold at your own pace, whether indulging in a spa treatment, exploring the powder-white beaches of Diani, or simply lounging beside the pool with a book in hand. Evenings bring golden sunsets, fine dining, and the comforting knowledge that every detail of your stay has been anticipated.

M&H Boutique Hotel invites you to step into a world of understated luxury and authentic Kenyan hospitality, a place where each visit becomes a treasured memory, and every guest becomes part of our story.
