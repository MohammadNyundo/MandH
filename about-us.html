About us

The retreat of your dreams

M&H Boutique Hotels

Where timeless elegance meets the gentle rhythm of the Kenyan coast.

Opened in 2024, this exclusive retreat was conceived as a sanctuary for the discerning traveler — a place where
the vibrant spirit of Diani Beach meets the tranquility of a private, world-class escape. Every element, from the
refined architecture to the thoughtful amenities, has been designed to inspire relaxation, connection, and
unforgettable moments.

At the helm is Margret Mweya, founder and Managing Director, whose career in hospitality and hotel bookings
spans decades. Margret’s deep understanding of guest expectations, coupled with her unwavering attention to
detail, has shaped M&H Boutique Hotels into a destination where service is as warm as the coastal sun and every
experience is elevated with grace.

For Margret, this hotel is more than an enterprise — it is the fulfillment of a cherished dream and the foundation
of a lasting legacy for her children, grandchildren, and generations to come. She has poured her heart into
creating an environment where every guest feels not only welcomed, but truly valued.

Here, you mornings might begin with a leisurely breakfast prepared with the freshest local ingredients. Days
unfold at your own pace — whether indulging in a spa treatment, exploring the powder-white beaches of Diani,
or simply lounging beside the pool with a book in hand. Evenings bring golden sunsets, fine dining, and the
comforting knowledge that every detail of your stay has been anticipated.

M&H Boutique Hotel invites you to step into a world of understated luxury and authentic Kenyan hospitality — a
place where each visit becomes a treasured memory, and every guest becomes part of our story.
